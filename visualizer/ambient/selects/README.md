# Selects

**Output Layer 03.** It earns a slot in what the panel plays when nothing else
is running.

The Periphery Center's Selects are hardened works that enter the neighborhood
with some permanence — "fewer, heavier, real." The POP equivalent for work made
of light rather than objects: the piece stops being something I launch and
becomes something that is simply *on*. In the room. Part of the place rather
than part of the practice.

That makes the tier self-limiting in an honest way, because the slots are
genuinely scarce — there's one wall — and because living with something for a
month surfaces what a demo never will. A piece that's beautiful for ninety
seconds and tiresome by day three doesn't belong here, and the only way to find
that out is to let it run.

Promote by `git mv`-ing the file up from `../studies/` and adding a line to
`/CYCLE.md` at the repo root.
