## [Notes from a Practice]

*A half-meter wall of light, learning to notice a hand in the room.*

There's a 256×256 LED panel on my wall — a physical thing, not a screen in the
usual sense, close enough that a single lit pixel still reads as one pixel and
never dissolves into a glow. This is where I'm teaching it to notice things: a
pointing finger, a spoken sentence, a face. Some pieces respond to you. Others
run on their own, generative and unattended. Both are the same practice.

This page is the map. Four places, four different speeds.

### Follow along

**[GitHub →](https://github.com/just4letters/01_LEDControl)**
Everything, as it actually is. Every sketch, every commit message, no polish
applied before it lands here. If you want to build your own version of
anything on this page, the code is the real documentation.

**[Instagram →](https://www.instagram.com/just_4letters/)**
The sketchbook. Short clips, posted the day they're made, one or two sentences
of caption. Half of these are failures that turned out to be more interesting
than what I was trying to build.

**[YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@just4letters)**
The evidence. Fewer, longer videos — always the panel on the wall, in the
room, with a hand in frame. This is the only place that shows what the thing
actually is: a physical object, not a screen recording.

**[The writing ↓](#entries)**
Longer notes, posted irregularly, whenever a piece resolves into something
worth explaining properly — the rules behind it, what it did that I didn't
design, what I got wrong first.
